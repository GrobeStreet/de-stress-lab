## Credential requests

Request only the level supported by actual work:

1. **Public audit record:** permission to state that the audit occurred and
   identify the repository and reviewed commit.
2. **Acknowledgment:** appropriate when the work materially improved a release
   or manuscript, using wording chosen or approved by the research team.
3. **Reference or testimonial:** appropriate only after the recipient has
   directly observed the quality, reliability, and communication of the work.
4. **Co-authorship:** only when contribution meets the venue's authorship rules
   and the full author group agrees.
5. **Affiliation:** only after a formal written appointment, visitor status,
   contract, or institutional agreement. A pilot, acknowledgment, or reference
   never creates an affiliation.

Never ask a researcher to sign off on the mathematics by virtue of title.
Invite them to reproduce, criticize, or delimit specific work.

## Commercial follow-through

After a complimentary pilot is successfully delivered:

- offer remediation implementation as a separately scoped project;
- offer per-release or monthly monitoring;
- offer manuscript/release red-team work;
- document price, acceptance criteria, and ownership before starting;
- never condition corrections, attribution, or report access on payment.
