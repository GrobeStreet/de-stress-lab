# Cover letter — Physical Review D

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Anatomy of a 2–3σ Hint: An Independent
Stress Test of the DESI DR2 Evolving-Dark-Energy Preference”** as a Research
Article in *Physical Review D*.

The manuscript independently reimplements the published DESI DR2 compressed
likelihoods and then asks where the preference for evolving dark energy is
statistically fragile. It reproduces the published compressed-likelihood
improvement ladder, calibrates the global and direct time-variation statistics
with 5,000 null mocks, repeats all seven tracer deletions inside the null
simulation, performs a held-out prediction for the influential LRG2
measurement, and tests a one-parameter low-redshift supernova offset. A
full-CMB variant verifies that CMB lensing does not remove the observed
sensitivity to LRG2.

The central result is deliberately narrower than a discovery claim. The
compressed likelihood contains a calibrated CPL-specific hint of time
variation, but most of its strength is not retained without LRG2, and the
selected localization is ordinary among ΛCDM fluctuations with an equally
strong global preference. The work therefore contributes an independent
reproduction, a selection-aware diagnostic framework, and specific
falsifiable follow-up targets.

This is an independent implementation of published compressed inputs, not a
raw-data reanalysis and not an exact reconstruction of DESI’s PR4/ACT
full-CMB stack. Those boundaries, the post-selection caveats, the pending
human clean-room rerun, and the use of AI systems for implementation and
manuscript assistance are stated explicitly.

The canonical reproduction package and checksum manifest are archived at
<https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21632602>. The reusable software release is
archived separately at <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21633731>, published on
PyPI, and developed in the public repository at
<https://github.com/GrobeStreet/de-stress-lab>.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere. No related submission or
transfer history is being reported. No recommended or excluded referees are
proposed at this stage.
