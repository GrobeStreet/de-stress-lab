# Reproducibility and statistical red-team work

The Dark-Energy Stress Lab is the flagship demonstration of an independent,
publish-regardless audit: reproduce the headline result first, then look for
where it is fragile, calibrate every selected statistic inside the null, and
state in advance what future evidence would change the conclusion.

## Engagements

### Reproduction sprint

- reconstruct the computational environment and data provenance;
- reproduce headline tables, figures, and test statistics;
- compare independent and author-provided implementations;
- deliver a discrepancy ledger with machine-verifiable outputs.

### Statistical red team

- influence and leave-group-out diagnostics;
- null simulation and selection-aware calibration;
- nuisance-parameter and measurement-swap tests;
- optimizer, prior, parameterization, and covariance stress tests;
- claim ladder separating reproduction, anomaly, diagnosis, and discovery.

### Prediction-ledger design

- convert narrative claims into dated, falsifiable thresholds;
- define data sources and adjudication rules before results arrive;
- freeze the ledger with hashes and versioned releases;
- publish later scoring without rewriting the original forecast.

## Deliverables

Every engagement is scoped around a reproducible repository, a concise findings
report, a test ledger, and a handoff session. Public work is preferred. Private
work can be considered when data or manuscripts are confidential, with a
redacted public methods record where permitted.

This is adversarial quality assurance, not purchased endorsement. Findings are
reported whether they strengthen or weaken the original claim. Domain experts
retain responsibility for scientific interpretation and any regulated use.
